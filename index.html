Who we are

Family-owned, neighborhood-run

Tim's Pizzeria & Pub opened in May 2011, started by Tim Carrabine after decades of experience in the pizza business going back to his youth. It's stayed what it started as — a relaxed, sit-down corner spot in a residential part of Cuyahoga Falls, the kind of place where the bar's full, the pizza's stretched fresh, and the party room fills up on a Friday.

Full bar, party room, and back patio on site.
