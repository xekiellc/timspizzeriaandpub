Who we are

Family-owned, neighborhood-run

At the young age of 14, Tim Carrabine began his journey of making homemade pizza and traditional Italian cuisine inside his brother-in-law's family restaurant. Hard work was no stranger to him as he gathered years of knowledge and experience. His unique flair has captured the authentic taste of Italy. That's where he is today making dishes for you at the original Tim's Pizzeria & Pub.

In 1984 he opened Tomaso's Pizzeria in the State Rd Plaza inside Funsville USA. In 1985 he opened a second location in Kent. From there in 1990 to Firestone Park. In 1992 he moved his State Road location to Front St. In 2011 Tim reopened this location calling it the original Tim's Pizzeria & Pub. Here he offers homemade Italian food inside a full dining room/pub/patio.

Although Tim's name is on the building, his daughters have become a huge part of the success of Tim's and have every intention of continuing the family business for years to come. Family dining at its finest; with an atmosphere that includes daily specials. His original cuisine and authentic Italian dishes only bring you back for more. Welcome to Tim's Pizzeria & Pub!
